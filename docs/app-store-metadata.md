## Description (4000)
dumbTV turns your Plex library into a 1990s cable box. Instead of scrolling a
grid of thumbnails deciding what to watch, you turn it on and take what's on.

It's the anti-streaming app. There are no menus to get lost in, no "are you
still watching," no endless choice. You build channels once — Saturday morning
cartoons, a movie channel, a marathon channel — and dumbTV runs them as
continuous broadcasts, whether anyone's watching or not. Tune in and you join
the show already in progress, exactly like real TV.

HOW IT WORKS
• Build channels from your Plex shows and movies, in a web page you open from
  your phone or laptop — nothing to configure on the TV itself.
• dumbTV precomputes a two-week schedule. Tuning a channel is instant.
• Flip channels with your remote, keyboard, or a swipe. Type a number to jump.
• There's a printable, on-screen guide — channels down the page, what's on now,
  what's next.

THE RULES (they're the point)
• No pausing. No skipping. No resume prompts. It's live TV — what's on is on.
• Never transcodes. Direct play only, so seeking is instant.
• Never touches your Plex watch state — nothing gets marked watched, nothing
  lands in Continue Watching.

SELF-CONTAINED
dumbTV runs entirely on your device and your network. It talks only to your own
Plex server; your token stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Works on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Mac. Requires a Plex server on your
network. Comes with a built-in demo lineup so you can see it working before you
link anything.
