Settings

PIN

—

Gates channel and rule edits so kids on the network can't rearrange the lineup. The TV never asks for it. Forgot it? Reset dumbTV's data on the device it runs on.

Timezone

—

The schedule is anchored to local midnight. A headless Pi often boots on UTC — set the zone so 08:00 means 8am where the TV is.

Display

How the picture fills the screen, and whether captions show. Applies to the TV.

On the TV, C toggles captions and the picture setting drives mpv panscan. Browser captions depend on the file exposing subtitle tracks.

AI assist —

Optional. Point dumbTV at a local Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint to get channel suggestions. It only proposes — you always review and apply.

Nothing here is ever in the playback path — the TV works whether or not this is set.

Backup & restore

Save the whole lineup — channels, sources, and rules — as one JSON. No token or PIN is included.

Importing replaces the current lineup. Good for backups, sharing a setup, or moving it to the Pi.
