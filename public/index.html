Each channel runs a continuous broadcast built from your Plex library. Pick what goes on it, decide how it's ordered, and it plays whether anyone is watching or not.

Commercials

Drop video files into the media folder and they become the ad breaks between shows. Sub-folders turn into tags, so media/ads/90s/toys/ tags everything inside it 90s, toys.

Durations are read with ffprobe. Anything it can't read gets skipped and listed, because a wrong duration makes the whole schedule drift.

Import from Plex

Point Cathode at a Plex library of commercials and it pulls them straight into the ad pool — nothing to copy onto the device. Make a library of ad clips in Plex, pick it here, and enable “Run commercials” on your channels.

Everything imported comes in as a commercial. Re-run after adding spots in Plex to pull the new ones.
