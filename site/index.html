What makes it dumbTV

NO MENUS

Turn it on, take what's on.

JOIN IN PROGRESS

Every channel is always mid-broadcast.

NO PAUSING, NO SKIPPING

It's live — that's the point.

HANDS OFF YOUR LIBRARY

Never transcodes, never touches your watch state.

SELF-CONTAINED

Your device, your network. No cloud, no account, no tracking.

EVERY SCREEN

iPhone, iPad, Apple TV & Mac. Configure from any browser on your network.

How it works

1

Install dumbTV; it shows a setup URL + QR code.

2

Open that on your phone, link Plex, build a channel.

3

Flip channels and watch. There's always something on.

Requires a Plex Media Server on your network. Comes with a built-in demo lineup so you can see it working first. Full setup guide →

Get dumbTV

One app for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Mac. Self-hosters: the same broadcast engine runs on a Raspberry Pi or Windows, straight from the repo.
