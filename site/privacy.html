dumbTV — Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026.

dumbTV does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

What the app talks to

dumbTV communicates only with servers you direct it to:

  • plex.tv — only during sign-in, to complete Plex's standard PIN link flow and discover your servers.
  • Your own Plex Media Server — to list your library and play your media, on your network.

Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

What's stored, and where

Your Plex authentication token and your channel configuration are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Watch state

dumbTV never modifies your Plex watch state — it doesn't mark anything watched and adds nothing to Continue Watching.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. The content shown comes entirely from your own Plex library.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
