dumbTV — Support

dumbTV turns your Plex library into a 1990s cable box. There's always something on — you flip channels and take what's playing.

Requirements

A Plex Media Server on your network. No Plex? The app ships with a built-in demo lineup so you can see how it works first.

Setting up

  1. Open dumbTV. Until it's configured, it shows a SET UP card with a URL and a QR code.
  2. On your phone or laptop, open that URL (or scan the QR).
  3. Go to Server and link Plex — it's the standard PIN flow at plex.tv/link.
  4. Build a channel: pick shows or movies, choose how they're ordered, and it starts broadcasting.
  5. Back on the TV, flip channels and watch.

Frequently asked

Why can't I pause or skip? By design — it's live TV. What's on is what's on. (Recording/playback is a separate, deliberate exception.)

How do I change channels? Remote or arrow keys (Apple TV / Mac), or swipe up and down (iPhone/iPad). Type a number to jump to a channel.

Something won't play. dumbTV only direct-plays — it never transcodes. A file your device can't play directly is skipped rather than transcoded.

It didn't mark my show watched. Correct — dumbTV never touches your Plex watch state.
